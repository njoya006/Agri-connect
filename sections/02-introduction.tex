\section{Introduction}

\subsection{Project Background}
Agriculture is important for many economies. Small farmers face challenges with record-keeping and market access. AgriConnect helps solve these problems with digital tools.

\subsection{Problem Statement}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Farmers use paper records that are hard to analyze
    \item Buyers and sellers cannot connect directly
    \item Market prices are not easily available
    \item Inventory tracking is manual and error-prone
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Project Objectives}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=*]
    \item Create digital farm management system
    \item Build peer-to-peer marketplace
    \item Provide real-time price information
    \item Implement inventory tracking
    \item Develop analytics dashboard
\end{enumerate}
